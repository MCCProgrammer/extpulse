    Overview

    The extended edition of Letterboxd: watchlist roulette, Fav Match, friends’ ratings, where to stream and shortcuts to friends.

    Director’s Cut is the extended edition of Letterboxd: the features you wish the site had, built right into the pages you already use. 🎲 WATCHLIST ROULETTE Can’t decide what to watch? Spin your watchlist, any Letterboxd list, or a friend’s watchlist. Pick from their whole list or only the films you both want to see, with a spin animation and confetti when a film is chosen. 🎬 FILM DETAILS IN ONE WINDOW Click any film to see its rating, how many members watched it, runtime and synopsis without leaving the page. Add it to your watchlist, like it or rate it from the same window. 📺 WHERE TO STREAM See where a film is streaming in your country, powered by JustWatch. Choose from 40+ countries and optionally show rent, buy and disc options too. 👥 FRIENDS’ RATINGS See which of the people you follow watched the film, with their star ratings, and who has it on their watchlist. 💚 FAV MATCH On any profile, find the members who share the most favorite films with it. Quick scan for fast results or Deep scan to reach up to 25,000 fans per film, with live progress and results you can filter. ⭐ KEEP YOUR FRIENDS CLOSE Pin your friends’ profiles and your favorite pages to the top right of every Letterboxd page. Search the people you follow with their profile pictures, add any link, and reorder everything. 🎯 GUESS THEIR RATINGS A quick game on any member’s profile: guess how they rated their films, then challenge friends to beat your score. ⚙️ MAKE IT YOURS Every feature can be turned on or off, and a short welcome guide helps you get started. PRIVACY No account, no analytics, no ads and no developer server. Your settings stay in your browser. The extension uses your existing Letterboxd session and never sees your password. Director’s Cut is free. Bug or idea? Write to contact@mccprogrammer.com. If it helps you, you can support it at https://ko-fi.com/mccprogrammer ☕ Director’s Cut is an independent project and is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by Letterboxd.
