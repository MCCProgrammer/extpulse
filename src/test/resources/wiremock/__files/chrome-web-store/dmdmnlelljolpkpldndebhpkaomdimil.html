    Unified Inbox for Gmail

    ExtensionCommunication37 users

    Overview

    Every Gmail account in one inbox — built right into Gmail. No new app to learn.

    Every Gmail account in one inbox — built right into Gmail. No new app to learn. No separate window, no new interface. Unified Inbox adds an "All Inboxes" view inside Gmail itself: connect your personal, work and side-project accounts and see every conversation in a single list, each one marked with its account's colour. Everything else works the way Gmail always has. ONE INBOX FOR EVERY ACCOUNT • "All Inboxes" in Gmail's sidebar, plus one entry per account • Account tabs with unread counts, just like Gmail's category tabs • Choose which categories each account shows (Primary, Promotions, Social, Updates, Forums) • Sort newest first, oldest first, or grouped by account — in the order you choose • Give each account its own name and colour, and reorder them by dragging • Open Gmail straight into All Inboxes if you like READ AND REPLY WITHOUT SWITCHING • Conversation view that looks and works like Gmail • Reply, Reply all and Forward from the right account automatically • Compose new emails and pick which account sends them — your Gmail signature included • Attachment previews for images, PDFs, video and text FIND THINGS FAST • Gmail's own search box searches every account at once, with Gmail operators (from:, has:attachment, older_than:…) • Choose what search covers: all mail in every account, or just the account and categories in your current view • Verification codes are detected and copied in one click — right from the list NEVER LOSE TRACK • Snooze conversations until later today, tomorrow, the weekend or any time you pick • Schedule send with Gmail's own scheduler, and save drafts to any account • "Remind me if nobody replies" — the conversation comes back to your inbox after 1, 3 or 7 days without a reply • Reusable templates for the replies you write again and again • Desktop notifications for new mail, coloured by account ORGANISE • Archive, delete, mark as read/unread, star, mark important, report spam, unsubscribe • Drag conversations onto a label, or right-click for every action • Create, rename, recolour and remove labels for each account • Undo for archive, delete and move • Select all conversations across pages, keyboard shortcuts (j/k, e, #, s, b, r, a, f, c, /) PRIVATE BY DESIGN • Runs entirely in your browser — there is no Unified Inbox server • Your emails travel only between your browser and Google • No analytics, no ads, no tracking • Remove an account or revoke access at any time Unified Inbox is free.
